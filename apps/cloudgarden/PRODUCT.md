# Cloudgarden

A local historical trading strategy evolution game for its owner. The product explores whether bounded parameter inheritance and ecological selection can yield strategies that later survive independent evaluation. It is not a live trading system.

The user explicitly chose a light garden aesthetic, an isometric surface, readable game HUD and clickable plants. Plant inspection must expose effective trading parameters, source provenance, ecology genes, ancestry, measured results and limitations. Exact coordinates follow the selected gene plane; canopy separation must preserve visible root anchors.

The full local SOL minute archive repeats indefinitely. Population and capital persist; monthly external contributions are 100 EUR at a disclosed fixed simulation exchange rate. Contributions are never counted as trading profit. Births use free capital rather than fixed planting slots; a 200 living plant safety ceiling bounds computation. The local gene collection holds inspectable strategy modules and user snippets as inert research text.

Historical-only evaluation cannot establish an independently profitable strategy. Repeated archive passes are training, not decades of new evidence. Trade decisions and mutations may use only then-available observations. Frozen holdouts and champion promotion remain separate evidence gates.
