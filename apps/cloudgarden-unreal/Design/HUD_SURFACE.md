# Native HUD refinement brief

Mode: Operate, layered over the game experience. Scope: HUD only. Plant art, environment and trading behavior are outside this refinement.

Visual authority: user-provided `HUD-reference.png`, also attached in the conversation as Cloudgarden Pflanzen-HUD mit EVO AN. Preserve its restrained mint accents, translucent deep sage surfaces, compact top bar, left portfolio rail, right ranking and wide bottom selected-plant panel. The reference overrides generic prohibitions on transparent panels. Figures in the image are illustrative and never data sources.

User correction, 2026-11-03: the opaque green rounded boxes are a rejected interpretation, not an approved incumbent design to preserve. Left/right rails and header must remain visually open, with fine dividers and restrained neutral shading for contrast. The bottom inspector is one integrated glass surface: actual background blur, low-opacity neutral charcoal tint, thin mint light edge. Merely lowering the opacity of a green box is not glass. Runtime Light/Regular typography replaces the coarse Canvas bitmap font. Do not call the result reference-faithful until the rendered composition supports that claim. The current procedural garden is explicitly a placeholder, not completed environmental art.

Latest three-image clarification: `Cloudgarden Strategy-Atlas.png` is the preferred light, open HUD direction, but its landscape is explicitly rejected as the game background. `Futuristisches Cloudgarden-Dashboard im Frühlingsgarten.png` demonstrates an unobstructed garden with selected-plant callout. `Cloudgarden Pflanzen-HUD mit EVO AN (1).png` demonstrates the expanded bottom plant view, including the actual plant portrait at the lower left. These are states of one HUD, not separate floating windows. Default: open header/rails/footer without a permanent bottom box. Selection: callout tied to the actual projected plant location. Details: expand the existing bottom region for portrait, measurements, chart and parameters; longer analysis and the gene library use tabs/scrolling inside that same drawer. Remove the central inspector window. Shading must be minimal, soft and curved/elliptical, never strong black vertical masks. User imagery remains visual authority; illustrative financial values remain excluded.

Current product scope: SOL only; one research plant with evolution paused; future concurrent strategy ceiling50. Do not display two gardens, EVO AN when paused, fabricated win rates, or a 400-specimen limit. Existing disappearance behavior is explicitly deferred while graphics are prioritized.

Required hierarchy: large legible NAV, secondary actual trading PnL/deposits/costs, compact date/cycle/playback controls; selected identity and paper/evolution state, actual plant statistics, measured curve with axes, effective settings with units. No raw JSON in the primary interaction. Advanced data must use grouped readable rows with working scrolling and no embedded-newline overlap.

Native desktop verification sizes: 1280x800 and 1920x1080. Body text should remain about14 logicalpixels, labels12minimum, strong contrast across translucent surfaces. Active/focus/hover/disabled/loading/empty/error states must be understandable. Unsupported controls are omitted rather than decorative. Mouse selection and keyboard I/G must preserve actual selection and values.

Motion weighting: Jakub production polish primary, Emil frequency restraint secondary. Mouse-origin panel changes may use a short180ms easing-out opacity response. Keyboard actions and repeated numerical updates are instant. macOS reduced-motion preference and the manual motion-off mode retain complete static functionality. No looping indicators, counter tweening or large camera movement.

Bounded verification: one batched desktop review of the primary and detail states at both sizes, one combined correction, at most one confirmation pass. A detector pass cannot establish visual quality or interaction correctness.
